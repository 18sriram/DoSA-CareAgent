How a patient's information moves

  1. The patient speaks in their own language. The audio is sent to the speech provider (Gnani). Our server deletes its copy straight after and keeps only the text.
  2. The language model (Groq) receives the text of the answers and returns a short summary and symptom tags. It does not decide urgency or give advice.
  3. Fixed rules, not an AI, check every sentence for emergency wording and suggest an urgency level.
  4. The doctor decides. Urgency, prescriptions and every alert wait for the treating doctor's confirmation.
  5. Everything is logged in the audit log with who did what.

For the hospital's data protection officer: steps 1 and 2 send patient audio and text to two outside companies. Before real patients use this, sign data processing agreements with them, or run the models inside the hospital's own network.
